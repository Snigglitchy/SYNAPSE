How SYNAPSE works

Everything you see starts from real, current PubMed papers — not a fixed dataset.

  1. Start from the overview, search a concept, or switch domains. The toggle above lets you scope everything to glioblastoma, neurodevelopment & neural stem cells, or both at once.
  2. Click any node to see the actual papers, journals, and years behind it — every link goes straight to the real PubMed page.
  3. "Explore neighborhood" drills one level deeper. If you're already inside a concept, this shows the shared ground between it and whatever you click next — not just a fresh search.
  4. Pin anything interesting, then select two or more pins to build your own map and export it as SVG, PNG, or PDF.
Gene/drug frequencies and connections are computed live from PubMed abstracts. Gene summaries and GO terms come from MyGene.info. The "papers at a glance" overview and relationship explanations are AI-generated syntheses — useful for quick orientation, not a substitute for reading the primary literature.
